Privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

CodeLab C & C++ is a programming learning application developed by ZamCan Nexus. Contact: zamcannexus@gmail.com.

Guest learning

Guest progress is stored locally on your device. It is not synchronized to an online account and can be lost when app data is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or the device is lost. You may choose to copy eligible guest progress to a signed-in account.

Google sign-in and Appwrite

If you choose Google sign-in, the authentication provider may provide your account identifier, name and email to Appwrite, which manages your account session. CodeLab uses your verified account identifier to associate private learning progress with your account. Eligible progress synchronizes when a connection is available; unsynchronized changes may not yet be recoverable on another device.

Learning records and certificates

We process completed lesson checkpoints and progress for synchronization. If certificate examinations are enabled, exam identity, answers, scores, attempt metadata and certificate verification status may be processed through Cloudflare. Certificate issuance is currently unavailable during preview. A displayed certificate template is not a valid credential.

Notifications and advertising

Local study reminders may be scheduled on your device. If you opt into push notifications, device notification tokens and delivery information may be processed by the messaging provider. Advertising services, when enabled, may process device and advertising information subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements.

Service providers and security

Appwrite supports authentication and cloud learning records. Cloudflare supports web hosting and certification security. Google provides account sign-in. We use access controls and server-side checks to protect account-linked information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

Local guest data can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. For account data access, correction, or deletion requests, contact us by email. Some records may need to be retained temporarily for security, dispute handling, or legal requirements. Do not send passwords or authentication secrets in email.

Changes

We may update this policy as features and providers change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
