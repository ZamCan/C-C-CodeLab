# CodeLab academic references and brand-use policy

## Scope
CodeLab lessons and examples are original teaching material. This list recommends independent primary references and further reading; it is **not** evidence that each source was consulted for every lesson, that textbook content was licensed for reproduction, or that any author, standards body, publisher or platform endorses CodeLab.

## Language standards and references
- ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 22/WG14 — C language working group: https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg14/
- ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 22/WG21 — C++ standard information: https://isocpp.org/std/the-standard
- cppreference — independently maintained C/C++ language and standard-library reference: https://en.cppreference.com/w/

## Books and additional learning
- Brian W. Kernighan and Dennis M. Ritchie, *The C Programming Language*, second edition. Publisher: Prentice Hall. This is a reading recommendation, not embedded licensed content.
- Bjarne Stroustrup, *Programming: Principles and Practice Using C++*. Author information: https://www.stroustrup.com/programming.html
- LearnCpp.com — independent online C++ tutorial: https://www.learncpp.com/

## Attribution and logos
- Display plain titles, author names, and neutral outbound links. Do not use publisher/book-cover/standards logos without verifying permission.
- Appwrite is the intended authentication/data service, and Cloudflare is the intended certificate/exam infrastructure. Neither is a sponsor or endorser absent written permission. Never imply a service is deployed solely because code or a logo is present.
- The Google G asset in `app/src/main/res/drawable/appwrite_google_g.xml` was copied from the owner's Appwrite-provided SVG. Its appearance does not establish a working OAuth flow. Follow Google identity branding rules when presenting a functional sign-in button.
- Cite individual sources within future lessons after editorial verification. Do not copy textbook passages or diagrams without authorization.

## Production acceptance before advertising launch
- Green CI on exact commit; Android APK compiled, installed, and exercised on physical device.
- Google OAuth browser flow, callback, account retrieval, logout, offline and failure states tested; Appwrite provider enabled and credentials confirmed securely.
- Cloudflare identity exchange and learner separation secured and tested before cloud progress or certificates.
- Real C and C++ offline compiler execution with stdout, stderr, exit codes and invalid-source diagnostics verified on device.
- Privacy policy, consent, AdMob test ads and release signing validated; no live ad IDs or secrets committed to source.
- Product and source attributions reviewed for correctness and trademark/license compliance.
